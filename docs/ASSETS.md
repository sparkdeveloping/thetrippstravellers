# Asset provenance

Original Natalie/Jordan photographs, attire boards and four outfit crops are copied unchanged from the approved Letter Home project available in this conversation. They illustrate the relationship and clothing references; they do not depict the future roora or a historic event date.

The Africa/Middle East coastline and Zimbabwe outline use Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 geometry (public domain), downloaded from the maintainer’s natural-earth-vector repository. Projection is equirectangular; the route is illustrative, with Dubai as the planned connection and Zimbabwe highlighted as a country, not an invented airport.

Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Geometry: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson

Existing aviation artwork is generated illustration. Fonts and licenses remain locally served.
